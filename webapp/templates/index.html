Why this matters: The latest availability forecast alone does not reveal how stable that expectation has been. Publication-to-publication revision behaviour provides an additional indication of whether expected availability is remaining consistent or changing frequently.
Methodology: The Stability Score is a custom analytical indicator derived from the historical share of unit availability revision records that remained unchanged between publications. The Availability Watch summarises the direction of net availability revision activity during the latest 24-hour window. These are analytical decision-support indicators, not official Elexon or NESO adequacy, reliability or system-margin measures.
